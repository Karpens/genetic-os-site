Questions

Which subjects are included?

Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Maths, Computer Science, Geography, Psychology, Economics and Business, at GCSE, AS and A2, for AQA, OCR and Edexcel. New subjects are added at no extra cost.

Which exam boards does this cover?

AQA, OCR and Edexcel. Choose your board and the topic list follows that board’s specification. Where a board doesn’t offer a subject at a level, we show you which boards do.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time, from your account. Cancelling stops future payments, and you keep access until the end of the period you've already paid for.

What is your refund policy?

Because you get full access to every subject the moment you pay, we don't offer refunds for time already paid for, including if you cancel partway through a period. By subscribing, you agree to this and to waiving the standard 14-day cooling-off period once you've started using the content. If something went wrong with your payment or you were charged in error, contact us and we'll look into it.

Can I use it for more than one exam board?

Yes — full access includes every board for every subject, so if a school moves you from one board to another, or you want to compare specifications, it's already there. Nothing extra to buy.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, the whole site — notes, questions, games and all — works in any phone or tablet browser. There's nothing to install.
