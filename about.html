About Full Marks

Revision for GCSE and A-level that follows your exact course, made by people who answer their emails.

What Full Marks is

Full Marks is a revision website for GCSE, AS and A-level students, with an iPhone app on the way. Pick your subject, exam board and level, and everything follows that specification: short notes for every subtopic, exam-style questions with an explanation for every answer, timed mock papers, games, and a study plan that gets you ready for your tests and your exams.

Who makes it

👋

Full Marks is a small, independent project, not a big company. It is run by its founder, who builds the site and reads every email and every mistake report.

That means if something is wrong, or could be better, you are talking to the person who can fix it. Write to hello@fullmarkss.com.

How the notes and questions are made

🎯Your exact specificationAQA, OCR A, OCR B, Edexcel and Cambridge, topic by topic, with the key words examiners look for.
💡Every answer explainedRight or wrong, you see why, so every question teaches you something.
🛠️Spot a mistake? We fix itEvery question has a Report button. Reports come straight to us, and the question is checked and corrected.
📈Honest predicted gradesThe predicted grade comes from your timed mock papers and practice. It is an estimate, not an official prediction, and we say so.

Safe for students

  • No ads and no tracking cookies, and we never sell personal data.
  • No messaging between students. Leaderboards show a racer name the student chooses, not their real name.
  • Teachers only see students who join their class with its code and whom they approve. A student can leave a class at any time.
  • Emails are only sent to students who turn them on, and every email has a link to stop them.
  • An account and everything in it can be deleted at any time.

For parents

  • One plan covers every subject and level. Payments are handled by Stripe, and you can cancel in two clicks.
  • Your child can send you a short report every Monday: how much they revised, their predicted grades and their tests coming up. You confirm it first, and you can stop it any time.
  • Not sure yet? Every new account gets a free week of full access, with no card.

For schools and teachers

Teachers and the students in their classes use Full Marks free: classes, homework, grades and each student's practice. For the whole school we set everything up for you, on one invoice.
