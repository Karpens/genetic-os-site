Safeguarding and data

  • Students join a class only with its code, and only after the teacher approves them. They can leave at any time.
  • Teachers see only the students in their own classes. Students never see each other's details or grades.
  • No messaging between students. Leaderboards show a racer name the student chooses, not their real name.
  • No ads, no tracking cookies, and we never sell personal data.
  • Class data is stored with Upstash in Frankfurt, Germany, and our server is hosted by Render. The full list of providers is in our privacy policy.
  • Any account, and everything in it, can be deleted on request.

Questions from your data protection officer or safeguarding lead? Email hello@fullmarkss.com and we'll answer them in writing.
