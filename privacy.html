Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Short version: we collect as little as we can. On the website your revision progress stays in your own browser. In the app it stays on your phone, unless you create an account so it follows you to other phones. We only store what we need to run your subscription, your account and the leaderboards, we never sell data, and there are no advertising cookies or tracking.

Who we are

Full Marks (fullmarkss.com) is a revision website and iPhone app operated from Slovakia. We are the controller of the personal data described here. Contact us at hello@fullmarkss.com for anything about your data.

Who we share data with

We use a small number of service providers who process data on our behalf, under contracts that protect it:

  • Stripe: payments, subscriptions, tax and invoices (Stripe privacy policy).
  • Render: hosts our server and the subscription database.
  • Upstash: stores leaderboards, mistake reports and app accounts’ saved progress (servers in Frankfurt, Germany).
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, if you choose it, and distribution of the app through the App Store.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website itself.
  • Google Fonts: serves the fonts, so your browser sends your IP address to Google when the page loads.
  • Our email provider, only to send sign-in links you ask for.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK and the European Economic Area, for example in the United States. Where they do, the transfer is covered by safeguards such as the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses and the UK addendum.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Cookies

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The site uses your browser’s local storage only to make it work (progress, settings and staying signed in). Stripe sets its own cookies on its checkout pages to process payments and prevent fraud.

How long we keep data

  • Subscription records: while your subscription is active, then for as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • App accounts and saved progress: until you delete your account. You can do that any time in the app (Me → Delete account) or by emailing us. Deleting your account doesn’t cancel a subscription; cancel that where you subscribed.
  • Leaderboards: weekly boards expire after about two months; all-time entries stay until you ask us to remove them.
  • Mistake reports: until the question has been reviewed, and at most the last 5,000 reports.
  • Server logs: kept only briefly by our hosting provider.

Children

Full Marks is for GCSE and A-level students, so many users are under 18. Anyone under 18 should ask a parent or guardian to buy a plan, and the parent or guardian is the customer. We don’t ask students for their age, real name or school, and we collect no personal data from students who don’t subscribe, create an app account or join a leaderboard.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and UK GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to how we use it, to receive it in a portable format, and to withdraw consent at any time. Email hello@fullmarkss.com and we will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Slovak data protection authority (Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov SR) or, in the UK, to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. If a change matters for subscribers, we will tell you before it applies.
