Calculations, fractions and percentages

Key points

  • Order of operations (BIDMAS): brackets, indices, division and multiplication, then addition and subtraction.
  • To add or subtract fractions, use a common denominator; to multiply, multiply numerators and denominators; to divide, multiply by the reciprocal.
  • Percentage increase/decrease: multiply by a decimal multiplier, e.g. a 15% increase means × 1.15, a 15% decrease means × 0.85.
  • Reverse percentages: divide by the multiplier, e.g. if £60 is the price after a 20% discount, the original was 60 ÷ 0.8 = £75.
  • Compound interest: final amount = initial × (multiplier)ⁿ for n years.

In more detail

  • Worked BIDMAS: 3 + 4 × 2² = 3 + 4 × 4 = 3 + 16 = 19, not (3 + 4) × 4 = 28.
  • Worked fractions: 2/3 + 1/4 = 8/12 + 3/12 = 11/12; 2/3 × 9/10 = 18/30 = 3/5; 3/4 ÷ 1/2 = 3/4 × 2/1 = 3/2 = 1½.
  • Worked mixed numbers: 2⅓ × 1½ = 7/3 × 3/2 = 21/6 = 3½ (always change to top-heavy fractions first).
  • Worked multipliers: £250 up 12% → 250 × 1.12 = £280; £250 down 12% → 250 × 0.88 = £220.
  • Worked reverse percentage: after a 20% discount the price is £60 → original = 60 ÷ 0.80 = £75 (not 60 × 1.2 = £72).
  • Worked compound interest: £2000 at 3% a year for 5 years → 2000 × 1.03⁵ = £2318.55.

3. A coat costs £60 after a 20% discount. What was the original price?

  1. £72
  2. £75
  3. £80
  4. £48

B: £75. 60 ÷ 0.8 = £75.

4. £1000 is invested at 5% compound interest for 2 years. What is the final amount?

  1. £1100
  2. £1102.50
  3. £1050
  4. £1105
Show answer

B: £1102.50. 1000 × 1.05² = £1102.50.

5. Work out 2/3 ÷ 4/9

  1. 8/27
  2. 1/2
  3. 3/2
  4. 6/12

C: 3/2. 2/3 × 9/4 = 18/12 = 3/2.

6. Work out 5 − 2 × (3 + 1)

  1. 12
  2. −3
  3. 8
  4. 0

B: −3. Brackets first: 5 − 2 × 4 = 5 − 8 = −3.

  1. £255
  2. £276
  3. £288
  4. £36

8. Write 3/8 as a decimal

  1. 0.38
  2. 0.375
  3. 0.83
  4. 2.67

B: 0.375. 3 ÷ 8 = 0.375.

9. Work out 2/5 + 3/4. Give your answer as a fraction.

  1. 11/20
  2. 6/20
  3. 23/20
  4. 5/9
Show answer

C: 23/20. Use common denominator 20: 8/20 + 15/20 = 23/20. Adding the numerators and denominators (5/9) is a common mistake.

10. A mass of 80 kg is decreased by 12%. What is the new mass?

  1. 70.4 kg
  2. 89.6 kg
  3. 9.6 kg
  4. 68 kg
Show answer

A: 70.4 kg. Multiplier is 0.88, so 80 × 0.88 = 70.4 kg. 9.6 kg is only the amount of the decrease, not the new mass.
