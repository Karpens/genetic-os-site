Topic S: Working scientifically and maths skills

Working scientifically and maths skills are tested throughout every chemistry paper, so this topic can earn you a lot of marks.

We will look at how models and theories develop, how to plan investigations and use apparatus safely, and how to present data in tables and graphs.

Then we cover uncertainty and evaluation, and the calculations you need: ratios, percentages, unit conversions and rearranging equations.

  1. S.1Models, theories and evidence in chemistryFree 25 questions
  2. S.2Planning investigations: variables, hypotheses and risk assessment37 questions
  3. S.3Practical techniques and apparatus (measuring, heating, gas collection, safety)73 questions
  4. S.4Presenting data: tables, graphs, significant figures and standard form28 questions
  5. S.5Uncertainty, accuracy, precision, repeatability and evaluating results36 questions
  6. S.6Chemistry calculations: ratios, percentages, unit conversions and rearranging equations44 questions
