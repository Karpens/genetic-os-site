GCSE Psychology · Edexcel

Edexcel GCSE Psychology revision

Every topic of the Edexcel GCSE Psychology (1PS0) specification in order, with notes and practice questions. Subtopics marked free have their notes and questions on this site.

Study Edexcel GCSE Psychology in Full Marks

Topic 1: Development

Development is about how children grow and learn, from the earliest brain development to the bonds they form with carers.

We will study theories of learning, like Piaget's stages and Dweck's mindset theory, and how the brain develops.

Then we look at attachment, including Bowlby's theory and Ainsworth's Strange Situation, and the effects of deprivation and day care.

  1. 1.1Development and learningFree 22 questions
  2. 1.2Early brain development and the effects of early experience48 questions
  3. 1.3Attachment: Bowlby's theory and Ainsworth's Strange Situation90 questions
  4. 1.4Deprivation, privation and the effects of day care45 questions

Topic 5: Social influence

There are 2 parts: we start with social influence, and finish with crowd behaviour, deindividuation, prosocial and antisocial behaviour, and the bystander effect.

  1. 5.1Social influenceFree 33 questions

Topic 6: Criminal psychology

Criminal psychology explores why people commit crimes, and how society responds.

We will study how crime is defined and measured, and biological and psychological explanations of offending.

Then we look at offender profiling, and ways of dealing with offenders and reducing reoffending.

  1. 6.1Defining crime and measuring crime: official statistics, victim surveys and self-reportFree 76 questions
  2. 6.2Biological explanations of offending: Lombroso, genetics and brain differences46 questions
  3. 6.3Psychological explanations of offending: Eysenck's personality theory and social learning46 questions
  4. 6.4Offender profiling: top-down and bottom-up approaches and case studies38 questions
  5. 6.5Dealing with offending: prison, restorative justice, anger management and recidivism51 questions

Topic 7: The self

The self explores how we see ourselves, and how our sense of self develops and affects our lives.

We will study self concept and self esteem, theories of the self, and how children develop self recognition.

Then we look at gender identity development, and how self esteem links to achievement and mental wellbeing.

  1. 7.1Self-concept and self-esteem: definitions, measurement and influencesFree 48 questions
  2. 7.2Theories of the self: Rogers, Cooley's looking-glass self and self-efficacy44 questions
  3. 7.3Development of the self-concept in children: Lewis and Brooks-Gunn's mirror (rouge) test28 questions
  4. 7.4Gender identity and sex-role development: biological, social learning and Kohlberg's explanations44 questions
  5. 7.5Applying the self: self-esteem, achievement and mental wellbeing43 questions

Topic 9: Sleep and dreaming

Sleep and dreaming explores why we sleep, what happens in our brains at night, and why we dream.

We will study the stages of sleep and E E G evidence, circadian rhythms and the body clock.

Then we look at the functions of sleep, sleep deprivation, sleep disorders and their treatments, and theories of dreaming.

  1. 9.1The stages of sleep, the sleep cycle and EEG evidenceFree 50 questions
  2. 9.2Circadian rhythms, the body clock and zeitgebers (Siffre and Aschoff and Wever)42 questions
  3. 9.3Functions of sleep, sleep across the lifespan and the effects of sleep deprivation40 questions
  4. 9.4Sleep disorders: insomnia, sleepwalking, narcolepsy and sleep apnoea, and their treatments63 questions
  5. 9.5Theories of dreaming: Freud's wish fulfilment and activation-synthesis, and research into dreams51 questions
