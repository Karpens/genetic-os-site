Reproduction and meiosis

Key points

  • Sexual reproduction involves fusion of male and female gametes, giving variation. Asexual reproduction involves one parent and gives identical clones by mitosis.
  • Meiosis halves the chromosome number, producing four genetically different gametes.
  • At fertilisation, gametes join and restore the normal chromosome number.
  • Some organisms use both, for example malaria parasites, some fungi, and plants with seeds and runners or bulbs (Biology only).

In more detail

  • Advantages of sexual reproduction: variation means a species is more likely to survive a change in the environment, and humans can use it in selective breeding.
  • Advantages of asexual reproduction: only one parent is needed, it is faster, it uses less energy, and many identical offspring can be made when conditions are good.
  • Meiosis in steps: chromosomes are copied, then the cell divides twice to form four gametes, each with a single set of chromosomes (23 in humans), all genetically different.
  • After fertilisation: the cell divides by mitosis, the number of cells increases, and as the embryo develops the cells differentiate.

1. How many cells does meiosis produce?

  1. 1
  2. 2
  3. 4
  4. 8

C: 4. Meiosis makes four genetically different gametes.

2. What is an advantage of sexual reproduction?

  1. It is faster
  2. It produces variation
  3. Only one parent is needed
  4. Offspring are identical
Show answer

B: It produces variation. Variation helps survival when the environment changes.

3. Asexual reproduction produces:

  1. Genetically different offspring
  2. Clones
  3. Gametes
  4. Only males
Show answer

B: Clones. Only mitosis is involved.

4. How many chromosomes does a human egg cell have?

  1. 23
  2. 46
  3. 92
  4. 22
Show answer

A: 23. Gametes have half the normal number.

5. What happens at fertilisation?

  1. Chromosome number halves
  2. Gametes fuse and restore the full number
  3. DNA is copied
  4. Mitosis stops
Show answer

B: Gametes fuse and restore the full number. The fertilised egg then divides by mitosis.

6. Which type of reproduction produces genetically identical offspring?

  1. Sexual reproduction
  2. Asexual reproduction
  3. Both equally
  4. Neither
Show answer

B: Asexual reproduction. Asexual reproduction uses mitosis, giving clones.

7. How many divisions occur in meiosis?

  1. One
  2. Two
  3. Three
  4. Four
Show answer

B: Two. Two divisions produce four haploid gametes.
