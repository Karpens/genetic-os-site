3.1 · Free notes

Differentiation: the gradient function of powers of x

Key points

  • The derivative dy/dx (or f′(x)) gives the gradient of a curve at any point; it measures the rate of change of y with respect to x.
  • Rule: if y = axⁿ then dy/dx = naxⁿ⁻¹. Constants differentiate to 0, and you can differentiate term by term.
  • Rewrite roots and fractions as powers first: √x = x^(1/2), 1/x² = x⁻². For example y = 4/x → y = 4x⁻¹ → dy/dx = −4x⁻² = −4/x².
  • To find the gradient at a point, substitute the x-coordinate into dy/dx.
  • The second derivative d²y/dx² is the derivative of dy/dx.

In more detail

  • Worked example: y = (x² + 1)/x = x + x⁻¹, so dy/dx = 1 − x⁻² = 1 − 1/x².
  • Meaning: if y is distance and x is time, dy/dx is velocity: the rate of change.
  • Exam tip: always rewrite in index form and expand brackets before differentiating; you cannot differentiate a product term by term.

Differentiation: the gradient function of powers of x: 10 questions

Pick an answer to check it, or open the answer for the explanation.

1. Differentiate y = x⁵.

  1. 5x⁴
  2. x⁴
  3. 5x⁵
  4. 4x⁵
Show answer

A: 5x⁴. Multiply by the power and reduce it by 1.

2. Differentiate y = 3x² − 4x + 7.

  1. 3x − 4
  2. 6x + 7
  3. 6x − 4
  4. 6x² − 4
Show answer

C: 6x − 4. 3 × 2x − 4 + 0.

3. What is the derivative of a constant?

  1. 1
  2. 0
  3. The constant
  4. x
Show answer

B: 0. A constant has zero gradient.

4. Differentiate y = √x.

  1. 2√x
  2. √x/2
  3. 1/√x
  4. 1/(2√x)
Show answer

D: 1/(2√x). x^(1/2) → ½x^(−1/2).

5. Differentiate y = 6/x.

  1. −6/x²
  2. 6/x²
  3. 6
  4. −6/x
Show answer

A: −6/x². 6x⁻¹ → −6x⁻².

6. Find the gradient of y = x³ − 2x at x = 2.

  1. 4
  2. 12
  3. 10
  4. 6
Show answer

C: 10. dy/dx = 3x² − 2 = 12 − 2 = 10.

7. What is d²y/dx² for y = x⁴?

  1. 4x³
  2. 12x²
  3. 12x³
  4. 24x
Show answer

B: 12x². dy/dx = 4x³, then 12x².

8. Differentiate y = (x + 3)².

  1. 2x + 3
  2. x + 6
  3. 2(x + 9)
  4. 2x + 6
Show answer

D: 2x + 6. Expand: x² + 6x + 9, then differentiate.

9. At which x is the gradient of y = x² − 6x zero?

  1. x = 3
  2. x = 6
  3. x = 0
  4. x = −3
Show answer

A: x = 3. 2x − 6 = 0.

10. Differentiate y = x^(3/2).

  1. (2/3)x^(5/2)
  2. x^(1/2)
  3. (3/2)x^(1/2)
  4. (3/2)x^(3/2)
Show answer

C: (3/2)x^(1/2). Multiply by 3/2 and subtract 1 from the power.
