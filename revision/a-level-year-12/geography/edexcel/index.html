Topic 3: Globalisation

Globalisation is about the growing connections between countries, and who wins and who loses from them.

We will measure globalisation, explain its causes, and study global governance and the global shift.

Then we look at transnational corporations, migration, the impacts of globalisation, and the global commons.

  1. 3.1Globalisation: features, measurement and indicesFree 42 questions
  2. 3.2Causes of globalisation: technology, trade liberalisation, deregulation and transport35 questions
  3. 3.3Global systems and governance: flows, interdependence, WTO, IMF, World Bank and trade blocs41 questions
  4. 3.4Global shift: newly industrialised countries and changing economic geography35 questions
  5. 3.5Transnational corporations: role, outsourcing and impacts on economies33 questions
  6. 3.6Migration and globalisation: flows, causes and consequences31 questions
  7. 3.7Impacts of globalisation: economic, social, cultural and environmental winners and losers36 questions
  8. 3.8Global commons, sustainability and responses: Antarctica, ethical consumption and resistance42 questions

Topic S: Geographical skills and fieldwork

Geographical skills and fieldwork are the methods geographers use to collect, analyse and present evidence.

We will plan fieldwork enquiries, collect primary data, and use maps, graphs and G I S.

Then we use descriptive statistics and statistical tests, and evaluate our fieldwork and its conclusions.

  1. S.1Fieldwork and data skillsFree 55 questions
  2. S.2The fieldwork enquiry process: questions, hypotheses, planning, risk assessment and ethics81 questions
  3. S.3Primary data collection and sampling: physical (coastal) and human fieldwork techniques73 questions
  4. S.4Cartographic and graphical skills: OS maps, choropleths, isolines, scatter graphs, kite and radial diagrams72 questions
  5. S.5Descriptive statistics: averages, range, interquartile range and standard deviation70 questions
  6. S.6Statistical tests: Spearman's rank, chi-squared, Mann-Whitney U and Student's t-test76 questions
  7. S.7GIS, ICT, remote sensing and qualitative data analysis75 questions
  8. S.8Evaluating fieldwork: reliability, validity, limitations and drawing conclusions79 questions
