Topic 1: Measurements and their errors

Measurements and their errors is the toolkit for every practical and calculation in A-level Physics.

We will look at SI units, types of error, precision and accuracy, and how to combine uncertainties when values are added, multiplied or raised to powers.

Then we use graphs, error bars and linearisation, essential maths skills, measuring instruments, and how to plan and evaluate investigations.

  1. 1.1Units, estimation and uncertaintyFree 15 questions
  2. 1.2Random and systematic errors, precision, accuracy, repeatability, reproducibility and resolution47 questions
  3. 1.3Combining uncertainties: absolute, fractional and percentage uncertainty34 questions
  4. 1.4Graphs: linearisation, error bars, and gradient and intercept uncertainties32 questions
  5. 1.5Mathematical skills: standard form, significant figures, logarithms, exponentials, trigonometry and small-angle approximations25 questions
  6. 1.6Planning investigations: variables, methods and risk assessment41 questions
  7. 1.7Evaluating methods and conclusions; research sources and referencing50 questions
  8. 1.8Measuring instruments and techniques: micrometer, calipers, oscilloscope, light gates and data loggers68 questions
