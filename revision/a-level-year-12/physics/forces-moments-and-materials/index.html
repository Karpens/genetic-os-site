Scalars, vectors and moments

Key points

  • Vectors have magnitude and direction; they can be added using scale drawings or by resolving into perpendicular components (Fcosθ and Fsinθ).
  • An object is in equilibrium when the resultant force and resultant moment are both zero.
  • Moment = force × perpendicular distance from the pivot. A couple is a pair of equal, opposite, parallel forces whose moment = force × perpendicular distance between them.
  • The centre of mass is the point where the object's weight appears to act; an object topples when its centre of mass falls outside the base.

In more detail

  • Worked resolving: a 200 N force at 35° above the horizontal → horizontal 200 cos 35° = 164 N; vertical 200 sin 35° = 115 N.
  • Worked vector addition: 6.0 N east + 8.0 N north → resultant 10 N at 53° north of east.
  • Inclined plane: weight resolves into mg sin θ down the slope and mg cos θ perpendicular to it; e.g. 50 N on a 30° slope → 25 N down the slope.
  • Worked moment: a 20 N force applied 0.30 m from a hinge at right angles gives a moment of 6.0 N m.

Scalars, vectors and moments: 10 questions

Pick an answer to check it, or open the answer for the explanation.

1. A 20 N force acts at 60° to the horizontal. What is its horizontal component?

  1. 10 N
  2. 17.3 N
  3. 20 N
  4. 34.6 N
Show answer

A: 10 N. Horizontal component = 20 cos60° = 10 N.

2. An object is in equilibrium when:

  1. The forces are large
  2. The resultant force and resultant moment are both zero
  3. It is moving
  4. It is at rest only
Show answer

B: The resultant force and resultant moment are both zero. An object moving at constant velocity can also be in equilibrium.

3. What is a couple?

  1. A single force
  2. Two equal, opposite, parallel forces that produce a turning effect
  3. Two forces acting at a point
  4. The weight of an object
Show answer

B: Two equal, opposite, parallel forces that produce a turning effect. A couple produces rotation with no resultant force.

4. Where does an object's weight appear to act?

  1. At its highest point
  2. At its centre of mass
  3. At its base
  4. At its edge
Show answer

B: At its centre of mass. This is the point used in moment calculations.

5. What is the moment of a 15 N force acting 0.4 m perpendicular from a pivot?

  1. 0.027 Nm
  2. 6 Nm
  3. 15 Nm
  4. 37.5 Nm
Show answer

B: 6 Nm. M = Fd = 15 × 0.4 = 6 Nm.

6. A force of 12 N acts at 30° above the horizontal. What is its vertical component?

  1. 6 N
  2. 10.4 N
  3. 12 N
  4. 20.8 N
Show answer

A: 6 N. Vertical component = 12 sin30° = 6 N.

7. Which of the following is a vector quantity?

  1. Mass
  2. Energy
  3. Displacement
  4. Speed
Show answer

C: Displacement. Displacement has both magnitude and direction, so it is a vector. Speed, energy and mass have magnitude only, so they are scalars.

8. Forces of 3.0 N and 4.0 N act at right angles to each other on an object. What is the magnitude of the resultant force?

  1. 7.0 N
  2. 1.0 N
  3. 5.0 N
  4. 12 N
Show answer

C: 5.0 N. For perpendicular vectors, R = √(3.0² + 4.0²) = √25 = 5.0 N. Adding the magnitudes (7.0 N) is only correct for forces in the same direction.

9. A 50 N force acts at 40° above the horizontal. What is its horizontal component?

  1. 50 N
  2. 38 N
  3. 32 N
  4. 65 N
Show answer

B: 38 N. The horizontal component is F cosθ = 50 cos 40° = 38 N. 32 N is F sinθ, the vertical component.

10. A boat heads directly across a river at 3.0 m/s relative to the water. The river flows at 4.0 m/s. What is the boat's speed relative to the bank?

  1. 3.5 m/s
  2. 7.0 m/s
  3. 1.0 m/s
  4. 5.0 m/s
Show answer

D: 5.0 m/s. The velocities are perpendicular, so the resultant speed is √(3.0² + 4.0²) = 5.0 m/s.
