Topic 1.3.2: Databases

Databases at A-level covers how to design, query and protect large collections of data.

We will use keys, entity relationship diagrams and indexing, and normalise data to third normal form.

Then we write S Q L to query, define and change data, keep data consistent with transactions and locking, and exchange data using X M L and J S O N.

  1. 1.3.2.1Relational databases: flat files, keys, entity-relationship diagrams and indexingFree 29 questions
  2. 1.3.2.2Normalisation to third normal form21 questions
  3. 1.3.2.3SQL queries: SELECT, WHERE, ORDER BY and JOIN24 questions
  4. 1.3.2.4SQL data definition and manipulation: CREATE, INSERT, UPDATE and DELETE22 questions
  5. 1.3.2.5Referential integrity, transaction processing, ACID and record locking30 questions
  6. 1.3.2.6Capturing, selecting, managing and exchanging data: XML and JSON25 questions
