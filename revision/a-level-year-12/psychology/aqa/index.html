Topic 8: Research methods: data handling and analysis

This topic is about what to do with data once a study has been carried out.

We will cover types and levels of data, averages and spread, graphs and distributions, then probability, significance and errors.

Finally we will choose and use inferential tests, including the sign test, and practise the maths skills that appear in every exam.

  1. 8.1Quantitative and qualitative data and levels of measurementFree 76 questions
  2. 8.2Descriptive statistics: measures of central tendency and dispersion59 questions
  3. 8.3Presenting data: tables, bar charts, histograms, scattergrams and distributions64 questions
  4. 8.4Probability, significance levels and Type I and Type II errors65 questions
  5. 8.5Choosing an inferential test and using critical values tables73 questions
  6. 8.6The sign test: calculation and interpretation63 questions
  7. 8.7Other inferential tests: Wilcoxon, Mann-Whitney, Spearman, Pearson, chi-square and t-tests73 questions
  8. 8.8Maths skills for psychology: ratios, percentages, decimals, standard form, significant figures and estimation60 questions
