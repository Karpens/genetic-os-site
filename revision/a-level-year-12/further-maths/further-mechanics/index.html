6.1 · Free notes

Momentum and impulse, including in vector form

Key points

  • Momentum = mv (kg m s⁻¹), a vector. Impulse I = change in momentum = mv − mu; for a constant force, I = Ft (N s).
  • Conservation of linear momentum: if no external impulse acts on a system, total momentum before = total momentum after.
  • In two dimensions, use vectors: I = m(v − u) with v and u as vectors; conserve momentum in each direction separately.
  • Impulse from a variable force is the area under a force–time graph (∫F dt).

In more detail

  • Worked example: a 0.5 kg ball moving at (6i − 2j) m/s is hit and moves at (−4i + 3j) m/s. Impulse = 0.5((−4i + 3j) − (6i − 2j)) = (−5i + 2.5j) N s, magnitude ≈ 5.59 N s.
  • Direction matters: take a positive direction and give velocities in the opposite direction a negative sign.
  • Exam tip: draw before-and-after diagrams with masses and velocities labelled.

1. What is the impulse needed to change the velocity of a 2 kg mass from 3 m/s to 8 m/s?

  1. 22 N s
  2. 16 N s
  3. 10 N s
  4. 5 N s
Show answer

C: 10 N s. 2(8 − 3).

2. A force of 20 N acts for 0.5 s. What is the impulse?

  1. 10 N s
  2. 40 N s
  3. 20 N s
  4. 0.025 N s
Show answer

A: 10 N s. I = Ft.

3. When is momentum conserved?

  1. Always
  2. Only in elastic collisions
  3. Only in 1D
  4. When no external impulse acts
Show answer

D: When no external impulse acts. Internal forces cancel.

4. A 3 kg ball moving at (4i + 2j) m/s receives impulse (−6i + 3j) N s. What is its new velocity?

  1. −2i + 5j
  2. 2i + 3j
  3. i + 3j
  4. 6i − j
Show answer

B: 2i + 3j. v = u + I/m = (4i + 2j) + (−2i + j).

5. A 0.2 kg ball hits a wall at 10 m/s and rebounds at 6 m/s. What is the magnitude of the impulse?

  1. 0.8 N s
  2. 16 N s
  3. 3.2 N s
  4. 2 N s
Show answer

C: 3.2 N s. 0.2(6 − (−10)) = 3.2.

6. What does the area under a force–time graph represent?

  1. Impulse
  2. Work done
  3. Power
  4. Acceleration
Show answer

A: Impulse. ∫F dt.

7. Two particles of 2 kg and 3 kg move at 5 m/s and −1 m/s and coalesce. What is their velocity?

  1. 2 m/s
  2. 0.4 m/s
  3. 3 m/s
  4. 1.4 m/s
Show answer

D: 1.4 m/s. (10 − 3)/5 = 1.4.

8. What are the units of impulse?

  1. N m
  2. N s
  3. kg m s⁻²
  4. J
Show answer

B: N s. Same as momentum.

9. A gun of mass 4 kg fires a 0.02 kg bullet at 400 m/s. What is the recoil speed?

  1. 8 m/s
  2. 0.5 m/s
  3. 2 m/s
  4. 20 m/s
Show answer

C: 2 m/s. 0.02 × 400 = 4 × v.

10. In 2D, how is momentum conserved?

  1. In each direction (component) separately
  2. Only in the direction of motion
  3. Only the magnitudes add
  4. It is not conserved
Show answer

A: In each direction (component) separately. Vector conservation.
