Topic 2: Ecosystems under stress

Ecosystems under stress explores how ecosystems work, and how human activity and climate change are threatening them.

We will study ecosystems as systems, global biomes, succession and biodiversity.

Then we examine tropical rainforests, Arctic tundra and coral reefs under stress, and how ecosystems can be managed and conserved.

  1. 2.1Ecosystems as systems: trophic levels, energy flow and nutrient cycling (Gersmehl model)Free 86 questions
  2. 2.2Biomes: global distribution, climate, soils and vegetation72 questions
  3. 2.3Succession and ecosystem change: seral stages, climax communities and disturbance77 questions
  4. 2.4Biodiversity: patterns, threats and ecosystem services33 questions
  5. 2.5Tropical rainforest under stress: deforestation, impacts and sustainable management25 questions
  6. 2.6Arctic tundra under stress: fragility, climate change and resource development79 questions
  7. 2.7Marine ecosystems under stress: coral reefs, bleaching, acidification and overfishing36 questions
  8. 2.8Managing ecosystems: conservation, protected areas and a local-scale ecosystem case study69 questions
