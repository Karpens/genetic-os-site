Topic 5: The water cycle and water insecurity

The water cycle and water insecurity explores how water moves around the planet, and why many people lack enough of it.

We will study the water cycle as a system, how it changes, and case studies of the Amazon and the Arctic.

Then we examine water insecurity, its causes, conflicts over shared rivers, and strategies for sustainable water supply.

  1. 5.1The water cycle as a system: drainage basin and global stores, flows and the water balanceFree 25 questions
  2. 5.2Changes in the water cycle: natural variability (ENSO), land use, urbanisation and extraction75 questions
  3. 5.3Water cycle case studies: Amazon rainforest and Arctic cryosphere, plus hydrograph and water budget analysis67 questions
  4. 5.4Water insecurity: physical and economic scarcity, water stress and water poverty29 questions
  5. 5.5Causes of water insecurity: population growth, climate, pollution and demand67 questions
  6. 5.6Water conflicts and transboundary rivers: players and geopolitics65 questions
  7. 5.7Sustainable water supply strategies: dams, desalination, groundwater, transfers and conservation40 questions
  8. 5.8Water management case study: large-scale versus community-based solutions60 questions

Topic 7: Climate change futures

Climate change futures looks at how the climate has changed in the past, and how it might change in the future.

We will study natural climate change, the evidence for it, and the human causes of recent warming.

Then we examine the impacts of climate change, future projections, and how the world can mitigate and adapt.

  1. 7.1Natural climate change: Quaternary and Holocene variations, orbital, solar and volcanic forcingFree 77 questions
  2. 7.2Evidence for climate change: ice cores, sediments, tree rings and instrumental records66 questions
  3. 7.3Human causes: enhanced greenhouse effect, radiative forcing and climate feedbacks70 questions
  4. 7.4Impacts of climate change on ecosystems, sea level, water, food and human health79 questions
  5. 7.5Climate projections: models, IPCC scenarios, uncertainty and risk66 questions
  6. 7.6Mitigation: emissions reduction, carbon trading, renewables and geoengineering34 questions
  7. 7.7Adaptation and international agreements: UNFCCC, Paris Agreement and resilience30 questions
