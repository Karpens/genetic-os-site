Topic 4.3: Emerging and developing economies

This topic looks at how emerging and developing economies grow and develop, and the barriers they face.

We will compare measures of development, examine the factors that hold countries back, and look at structural change and urbanisation.

Then we evaluate market based and interventionist strategies, multinationals, aid, debt relief, Fairtrade and sustainable development.

  1. 4.3.1Economic growth and development: measures including HDI, GNI per capita (PPP) and other indicatorsFree 47 questions
  2. 4.3.2Factors influencing growth and development: primary product dependency, savings and foreign currency gaps, demographics, debt, infrastructure and institutions50 questions
  3. 4.3.3Structural change, the Lewis model, urbanisation and the informal economy67 questions
  4. 4.3.4Market-oriented development strategies: trade liberalisation, privatisation and microfinance83 questions
  5. 4.3.5Interventionist development strategies: human capital, infrastructure, protectionism, buffer stocks and industrialisation70 questions
  6. 4.3.6Multinational corporations and foreign direct investment in developing economies30 questions
  7. 4.3.7Aid, debt relief, Fairtrade, NGOs and the role of the IMF and World Bank26 questions
  8. 4.3.8Sustainable development and environmental constraints on growth59 questions

Topic Q: Quantitative skills and synoptic evaluation

This topic practises the calculation and evaluation skills needed across the whole A level course.

We will work through business, distribution, international, macroeconomic and financial calculations.

Then we focus on synoptic essays and data response questions, and how to build strong chains of analysis and evaluation.

  1. Q.1Business calculations: revenue, costs, profit, elasticity, market share and concentration ratiosFree 32 questions
  2. Q.2Distribution and development calculations: Gini, Lorenz, deciles, index numbers and HDI63 questions
  3. Q.3International calculations: opportunity cost, terms of trade, exchange rates and balance of payments data62 questions
  4. Q.4Macro and financial calculations: multiplier, tax and debt ratios, real and nominal interest rates, bond yields47 questions
  5. Q.5Synoptic essay, data-response and evaluation technique across micro, macro and global themes62 questions
