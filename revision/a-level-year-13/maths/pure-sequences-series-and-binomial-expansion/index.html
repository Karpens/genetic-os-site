Sequences, series and binomial expansion

Key points

  • Arithmetic series: nth term a + (n − 1)d; sum Sₙ = (n ÷ 2)[2a + (n − 1)d].
  • Geometric series: nth term arⁿ⁻¹; sum Sₙ = a(1 − rⁿ) ÷ (1 − r); sum to infinity S∞ = a ÷ (1 − r) for |r| < 1.
  • Recurrence relations define each term from the previous one; sequences may be increasing, decreasing or periodic.
  • The binomial expansion extends to negative and fractional powers for |x| < 1, which is used to make approximations.
  • Sigma notation Σ compactly represents a sum of terms.

In more detail

  • Worked arithmetic series: 5 + 8 + 11 + … to 20 terms → S = 20/2 × (2 × 5 + 19 × 3) = 10 × 67 = 670.
  • Worked geometric series: 8 + 4 + 2 + … → S∞ = 8 ÷ (1 − ½) = 16, and S₁₀ = 8(1 − 0.5¹⁰) ÷ 0.5 = 15.98.
  • Worked sigma: Σ(3r − 1) from r = 1 to 10 = 3 × 55 − 10 = 155.
  • Worked binomial, fractional power: (1 − 2x)^(1/2) = 1 − x − ½x² − ½x³ − …, valid for |x| < ½.
  • Worked approximation: √0.98 = (1 − 2 × 0.01)^(1/2) ≈ 1 − 0.01 − 0.00005 = 0.98995.
  • Worked recurrence: uₙ₊₁ = 3uₙ − 1 with u₁ = 2 → 2, 5, 14, 41, …, an increasing sequence.

Sequences, series and binomial expansion: 10 questions

Pick an answer to check it, or open the answer for the explanation.

1. Find the sum to infinity of a geometric series with a = 4 and r = ½

  1. 2
  2. 6
  3. 8
  4. 16
Show answer

C: 8. S∞ = a ÷ (1 − r) = 4 ÷ 0.5 = 8.

2. What is the 10th term of the arithmetic sequence 3, 7, 11, ...?

  1. 36
  2. 39
  3. 40
  4. 43
Show answer

B: 39. a + (n − 1)d = 3 + 9 × 4 = 39.

3. For what values of r does a geometric series have a sum to infinity?

  1. |r| > 1
  2. |r| < 1
  3. r = 1
  4. All values
Show answer

B: |r| < 1. Otherwise the terms don't tend to zero.

4. What does a recurrence relation do?

  1. Gives the nth term directly
  2. Defines each term using the previous term(s)
  3. Sums a series
  4. Finds the limit
Show answer

B: Defines each term using the previous term(s). For example, uₙ₊₁ = 2uₙ + 1.

5. The binomial expansion for a negative power is valid when:

  1. |x| > 1
  2. |x| < 1
  3. x = 1
  4. x is an integer
Show answer

B: |x| < 1. Otherwise the infinite series doesn't converge.

6. What is the sum of the first 20 terms of 2, 5, 8, ...?

  1. 590
  2. 610
  3. 620
  4. 600
Show answer

B: 610. S = (20/2)[2(2) + 19(3)] = 10 × (4 + 57) = 10 × 61 = 610.

7. A geometric sequence has a = 3 and r = 2. What is the 5th term?

  1. 24
  2. 48
  3. 96
  4. 15
Show answer

B: 48. arⁿ⁻¹ = 3 × 2⁴ = 48.

8. For which value of r does a geometric series converge?

  1. r = 2
  2. r = −0.5
  3. r = 1
  4. r = −3
Show answer

B: r = −0.5. Convergence requires |r| < 1.

9. An arithmetic series has first term 5 and common difference 3. What is the sum of its first 15 terms?

  1. 390
  2. 345
  3. 195
  4. 780
Show answer

A: 390. S₁₅ = (15/2)[2(5) + 14(3)] = 7.5 × 52 = 390. The value 780 comes from forgetting to halve the bracket (the n ÷ 2 factor).

10. A geometric series has first term 2 and common ratio 3. What is the sum of the first 6 terms?

  1. 728
  2. 486
  3. 364
  4. 1458
Show answer

A: 728. S₆ = 2(3⁶ − 1)/(3 − 1) = 2 × 728/2 = 728. The value 486 is only the 6th term (2 × 3⁵ = 486), not the sum.
